# Numin — Economic Plan: Acquisition Offers
**For executive review · June 2026**

---

## Why now
Our cost of goods has dropped from **~90% to ~50%** of the subscription price.
That one change makes two acquisition offers viable that didn't pencil before —
one we've run successfully (an email-gated discount) and one we recently tested
that drove huge email volume (**Try One Dollar**).

At the same time, email capture has fallen since the site + price change —
**opt-in 6.2% → 2.7%**, and **~750 → ~350** new emails/month — so we need a
stronger offer back in market. This plan lays out the economics of both offers
and how we manage the risk.

---

## The economics, simply
- Subscription: **$54 / month** (20-pack)
- Cost to make + ship: **~$27** (50% of price; was ~$49 at 90% COGS)
- **Contribution per order: ~$27**
- Traffic: **~13,000 visitors/month**; each 1 pt of opt-in ≈ **~130 new emails/mo**
- Typical retention: **~4 months** *(the number we most need to confirm)*

---

## Offer 1 — The Comeback Offer (email-gated discount + subscribe & save)
An inflated anchor price with a deep first-order discount that the customer
unlocks by entering an email and subscribing. **This is the offer that filled our
list before.**

| | |
|---|---|
| Opt-in | ~5–7% (we ran ~6.2% before) → **~650–910 emails/mo** |
| First order (~$49) | **+$22 contribution** |
| Pays back on the first order? | **Yes**, at a reasonable CAC |
| Risk | **Low** |

**Why it works now:** at 90% COGS the discounted first order made about $0; at 50%
COGS it makes **+$22**. Same offer — now profitable from order one.

> **Note:** this is our steady, low-risk engine. It rebuilds the email list and
> makes money on the first order, before we count on anyone staying.

---

## Offer 2 — Try One Dollar
Customer pays **$1 today** and gets the product. If they don't cancel, they're
**charged the full price at day 21**; their **first refill ships at day 30**, then
monthly after that.

| | |
|---|---|
| Opt-in | ~20–33% (we saw **33%** on a simpler version) → **~2,600–4,300 emails/mo** |
| First order | **~$3–6 contribution** (thin) |
| Pays back on the first order? | **No — this is a bet on refills** |
| Risk | **High** |

**The upside:** by far our biggest list-builder — roughly **7× our current email
volume.**

**The risk, on a first-order basis:**
- We're profitable on the first order only if **about half of trial-starts convert
  at day 21** (break-even is ~49% conversion). At a healthy 55%, the first order
  throws off only **~$3**.
- So the first order **does not pay back the cost of acquiring the customer** — the
  return depends entirely on customers staying for refills (orders 2+).
- Every customer who takes the $1 and leaves costs us **~$26** (product shipped),
  plus whatever we paid to acquire them.
- The day-21 charge is the classic trigger for **chargebacks**. We must keep these
  under ~1% — clear disclosure up front, a reminder before the charge, and
  one-click cancel — or we risk our payment processing.

> **Why we can consider it now:** at 90% COGS we'd have needed ~90% of trials to
> convert at day 21 just to break even on the first order (impossible). At 50%
> COGS that break-even drops to ~49% (achievable). Lower COGS is what puts this
> offer back on the table.

---

## Side by side
| | Comeback Offer | Try One Dollar |
|---|---|---|
| New emails / month | 650–910 | **2,600–4,300** |
| First-order contribution | **+$22** | ~$3–6 |
| Pays back on order 1 | Yes (reasonable CAC) | No — refill bet |
| Risk | **Low** | **High** |
| Best for | Steady, profitable list growth | Aggressive volume |

---

## What we'll watch
- **Opt-in rate** vs. today's 2.7% (target back to ~6%+)
- **Try One Dollar — day-21 conversion** (need ≥~49% to break even on the first
  order; healthy ≥55%)
- **Reorder / retention rate** (orders 2+ — this is what makes Try One Dollar pay)
- **Chargeback rate** (keep < 1%)
- **CAC**, by offer

---

## Recommendation
1. **Run the Comeback Offer now.** Low risk, profitable on the first order, and it
   rebuilds the list we've lost.
2. **Test Try One Dollar on a portion of traffic** with the guardrails above. Scale
   only if day-21 conversion and reorder rates clear the bar.
3. **Pull the numbers from our last $1 campaign** — day-21 conversion, reorder
   rate, chargebacks — and replace the estimates here with our actual results
   before scaling.

---

## Assumptions & notes
- $54 sub, ~50% COGS, ~13K visitors/mo, ~4-month retention — to be confirmed.
- Opt-in: Comeback proven at ~6.2%; Try One Dollar observed at 33% on a simpler
  version, planned conservatively here at 20%.
- Try One Dollar's first-order math assumes returned product can't be resold
  (conservative) and ~55% day-21 conversion (unproven — confirm from last campaign).
- **"First-order basis"** deliberately ignores future refills — it shows the risk
  *before* we bet on retention. Refills are the upside, not the plan.
