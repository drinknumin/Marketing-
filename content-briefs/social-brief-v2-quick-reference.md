# Numin Social Brief V2: Quick Reference

The fast version. One screen per idea: what it is, a couple of phrases to spark a hook, and a one-line transition to the product. Full scripts, receipts, and generational lines live in the long brief. Use this to pick a topic and go.

**Transition rules (the whole playbook):**
- Land the topic first, then hinge to the product in your own words. Never read a set line.
- Vary how you name it: our founder is a neuroscientist, it is caffeine-free, it is built for decision fatigue. One angle per video, never the same words twice.
- Product in hand and the study on screen only on focus, decision, and crash topics. Keep both out of the sensitive ADHD-identity ones, and off plastics and food.
- Claims stay at focus and decision-making support, never "cures" or "fixes." Add once per caption: "These statements have not been evaluated by the FDA. This product is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease."
- Tag key: (product + study) say it with product in hand. (soft) light or optional. (sensitive) no product, no study, compassionate. (study-forward) lead with the published trial. (no product) skip the pitch.
