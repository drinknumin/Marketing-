---

## The science bridge (used in copy + the Numin mention)
From the book's "Science in 30 Seconds": the prefrontal cortex uses **glutamate** to make decisions. Over a day it accumulates faster than the brain clears it; the clearing protein (**GLT-1**) gets overwhelmed → degraded signaling, impaired judgment, "4pm brain fog." Sleep runs the deepest cleanup (glymphatic system).
**Caffeine Reality Check:** caffeine blocks adenosine (feels awake) but does *nothing* for glutamate. → Numin's honest role: it targets the root cause caffeine can't.

### PART A — Type you into a profile (2 questions)

**Q1 — Core tension (primary driver; each answer = +3 to that profile)**
*"When it comes to decisions, which hits closest to home?"*
- A. "If I lose my mental edge, I don't feel like myself." → **Cognitive Identity Protector**
- B. "Too many decisions are coming at me at once." → **Decision-Thread Manager**
- C. "Everything funnels through me — at work and at home." → **Load Accumulator**
- D. "My work leaves no room for error — I can't afford an off day." → **Precision-Cost Professional**
- E. "I want to understand the mechanism and optimize it." → **Self-Experimenter**
- F. "My brain works differently than it used to, and I want to adapt." → **Late-Career Recalibrator**

**Q2 — Behavioral confirm (each answer = +1 to that profile; ties broken toward Q1)**
*"It's 4pm and your brain is fried. What slips first?"*
- A. I start second-guessing calls I'd normally trust → Cognitive Identity Protector
- B. I freeze — too many open loops, can't pick what's next → Decision-Thread Manager
- C. I default to whatever's easiest just to get through → Load Accumulator
- D. I get sloppy on details I usually catch → Precision-Cost Professional
- E. I notice my focus drop and want to know why → Self-Experimenter
- F. It takes me longer to bounce back than it used to → Late-Career Recalibrator

> **Scoring:** Highest profile total wins. Q1 dominates; Q2 confirms/breaks ties.

### PART B — Score the fatigue (Dr. Watson's 10-item Self-Assessment, verbatim)
*Scale each: 1 = Never · 2 = Rarely · 3 = Sometimes · 4 = Often · 5 = Almost Always*

1. By late afternoon, I find it difficult to make even simple choices (e.g., what to eat for dinner).
2. I frequently default to "whatever is easiest" rather than what I actually want.
3. I procrastinate on important decisions, even when I know what I should do.
4. I make impulse purchases, especially online or in the evening.
5. I feel mentally exhausted at the end of the day, even if I wasn't physically active.
6. I find myself scrolling through options (Netflix, restaurants, products) without being able to choose.
7. I snap at family members or colleagues over minor issues, especially later in the day.
8. I struggle to start new habits or stick to goals I've set for myself.
9. I often regret decisions I made when I was tired or stressed.
10. I avoid situations that require multiple choices (e.g., shopping, planning, negotiating).

**Q (amplifier):** *"Do other people regularly depend on your decisions — at work, at home, or both?"*
- Yes → **+5** (the book's "Central Node Amplifier")
- No → +0

**Banding (out of 50, +5 possible):**
| Score | Level | What it means |

| 21–35 | **Moderate** | Decision fatigue is actively impacting your effectiveness. |

## THE 6 RESULT GUIDES (what people get)

### 1. Cognitive Identity Protector
- **Identity line:** "Your sharpness is part of who you are — which is exactly why a foggy 4pm rattles you."
- **#1 move:** **Mindfulness** (Ch. 10) — lower the *emotional* cost of decisions so a dip doesn't feel like a crisis.
- **Quick win today:** 3-minute breathing space, twice daily (mid-morning + mid-afternoon).
- **Toolkit they get:** Quick Reference Card + 30-Day Reset (Week 4 mindfulness track).

### 2. Decision-Thread Manager
- **Identity line:** "It's not that decisions are hard — it's that 40 of them are open at once."
- **#1 move:** **Decision Economy** (Ch. 9) — spend cognitive fuel on what matters; move your #1 decision to 9am.
- **Quick win today:** Build a Decision List — triage tomorrow's calls by cognitive demand.
- **Toolkit they get:** The Decision List template + Quick Reference + 30-Day Reset (Week 3).

### 3. Load Accumulator
- **Identity line:** "Everything funnels through you — at work and at home. That's a real cognitive tax."
- **#1 move:** **Default Architecture** (Ch. 9) — make the good choice the easy choice so you stop re-deciding.
- **Quick win today:** Install 3 defaults (standard breakfast, morning routine, outfit formula); lay out tomorrow tonight.
- **Toolkit they get:** Full toolkit — Self-Assessment, Decision List, 30-Day Reset (esp. Week 2 defaults + the household "Co-Node" rules).

### 4. Precision-Cost Professional
- **Identity line:** "You don't get to have an off day. The cost of a tired decision is too high."
- **#1 move:** **CMDT Training** (Ch. 10) — train neural efficiency so you hold up under load.
- **Quick win today:** Protect your peak hours for one-way-door (irreversible) calls; add a cognitive challenge to your next walk.
- **Toolkit they get:** Quick Reference (Bezos one-way/two-way doors) + 30-Day Reset (Week 4 CMDT).

### 5. Self-Experimenter
- **Identity line:** "You don't want platitudes — you want the mechanism."
- **#1 move:** **GLT-1 Deep Dive** (Ch. 8) — understand glutamate clearance, then optimize it.
- **Quick win today:** Track your best/worst decision times for 3 days (Daily Tracking template).
- **Toolkit they get:** Daily Tracking template + Quick Reference (the science) + 30-Day Reset.
- *(This profile is most receptive to the Numin mechanism — strongest place to test a heavier Numin mention later.)*

### 6. Late-Career Recalibrator
- **Identity line:** "Your brain works differently than it did at 30 — the win is working *with* it, not against it."
- **#1 move:** **Sleep + Cognitive Reserve** (Ch. 10) — activate the glymphatic cleanup that clears the day's glutamate.
- **Quick win today:** The 10-3-2-1 sleep rule; phone in another room tonight.
- **Toolkit they get:** Quick Reference (10-3-2-1 rule) + 30-Day Reset (Week 4 sleep track).
